Frequently Asked Questions

How can I schedule a property visit?

You can easily schedule a property visit by clicking the "Schedule Visit" button on any property card or detail page, selecting your preferred date and time, and submitting the form. Our executive will call to confirm.

Are all listed properties verified?

Yes! Every single property listed on Apex Realty goes through strict title checks, builder background verification, and clear documentation audits before going live.

Is the property price negotiable?

Many of our listed properties carry a "PRICE NEGOTIABLE" tag. You can directly discuss the best price with our dealer during the site visit or via phone/WhatsApp.

Do you assist with home loans?

Yes, we partner with top banks like SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank to help our buyers secure fast-track home loans with lowest interest rates.

Can I contact the dealer instantly via WhatsApp?

Absolutely! Click any WhatsApp button on property cards or use the floating WhatsApp widget to chat directly with Rajesh Sharma with pre-filled property queries.
